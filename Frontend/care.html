Plant Care Guide

Find the right care routine for your favourite plants.

🍃

Money Plant

Easy Care

💧 Water: When the top soil feels dry.

☀️ Sunlight: Bright, indirect light.

🌡️ Care: Avoid standing water.

Tip: Trim long stems to encourage fuller growth.

🌿

Snake Plant

Easy Care

💧 Water: When the soil is completely dry.

☀️ Sunlight: Indirect light or low light.

🌡️ Care: Avoid overwatering.

Tip: Use a pot with drainage holes to prevent water buildup.

🌵

Aloe Vera

Easy Care

💧 Water: When the soil is completely dry.

☀️ Sunlight: Bright light with some direct sun.

🌡️ Care: Use well-draining soil.

Tip: Avoid overwatering and choose a pot with drainage holes.

🤍

Peace Lily

Moderate Care

💧 Water: When the top soil begins to dry.

☀️ Sunlight: Bright, indirect light.

🌡️ Care: Avoid harsh direct sunlight.

Tip: Keep soil evenly moist without making it waterlogged. Toxic to pets if eaten.

🌴

Areca Plant

Moderate Care

💧 Water: When the top soil starts drying.

☀️ Sunlight: Bright, indirect light.

🌡️ Care: Avoid cold drafts and soggy soil.

Tip: Wipe dusty leaves gently with a soft, damp cloth.

🌱

Spider Plant

Beginner Friendly

💧 Water: When the top soil starts drying.

☀️ Sunlight: Bright, indirect light.

🌡️ Care: Keep soil lightly moist, not soggy.

Tip: Trim dry leaf tips and rotate the pot occasionally.

🌹

Rose Plant

Flowering Plant

💧 Water: When the top soil starts drying.

☀️ Sunlight: Around 6 hours of direct sunlight.

🌡️ Care: Provide well-draining soil and airflow.

Tip: Remove faded flowers and check leaves regularly.

🌺

Hibiscus

Flowering Plant

💧 Water: Keep soil evenly moist, not soggy.

☀️ Sunlight: Several hours of direct sunlight.

🌡️ Care: Use well-draining soil.

Tip: Regular sunlight supports flowering. Protect from extreme heat.

🌼

Jasmine

Flowering Plant

💧 Water: When the top soil starts drying.

☀️ Sunlight: Bright light with some direct sun.

🌡️ Care: Provide good drainage and airflow.

Tip: Prune lightly after flowering to maintain its shape.

🌿

Tulsi

Easy Care

💧 Water: When the top soil feels dry.

☀️ Sunlight: Around 4–6 hours of sunlight.

🌡️ Care: Use well-draining soil.

Tip: Pinch off flower tips when needed to encourage leafy growth.

🍃

Curry Leaf

Herb Plant

💧 Water: When the top soil begins to dry.

☀️ Sunlight: Around 4–6 hours of sunlight.

🌡️ Care: Use well-draining soil.

Tip: Keep the plant in a warm, sunny spot and avoid waterlogged soil.

🍋

Lemon Plant

Fruit Plant

💧 Water: When the top soil starts drying.

☀️ Sunlight: Around 6–8 hours of direct sunlight.

🌡️ Care: Use well-draining soil and a sunny spot.

Tip: Choose a pot with drainage holes to prevent water buildup.
